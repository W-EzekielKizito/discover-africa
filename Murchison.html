Murchison Falls National Park, Uganda

Where the mighty River Nile forces itself through a narrow 7-meter gorge.

About Murchison Falls

Murchison Falls National Park is Uganda’s largest national park. The highlight is the dramatic drop where the Victoria Nile forces its entire volume through a tiny rocky gap, plunging 43 meters below before flowing calmly into Lake Albert.

Key Highlights

  • Boat cruises along the Victoria Nile to the base of Murchison Falls.
  • Spotting elephants, Rothschild giraffes, lions, and Nile crocodiles.
  • Top of the falls hike for up-close viewing of the thunderous waters.
