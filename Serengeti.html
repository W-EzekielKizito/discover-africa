Serengeti National Park, Tanzania

Home to the world-famous Great Wildebeest Migration.

About Serengeti National Park

Spanning over 14,750 square kilometers, the Serengeti ecosystem is one of Africa’s most celebrated wildlife refuges. Millions of wildebeest, zebras, and gazelles move through its endless plains every year in search of fresh grazing grounds, closely trailed by apex predators like lions, cheetahs, and leopards.

Key Highlights

  • Witness the river crossings during the Great Migration (July to October).
  • Sunrise hot air balloon safaris with champagne breakfasts in the bush.
  • Game drives through Seronera Valley, home to large lion pride populations.
