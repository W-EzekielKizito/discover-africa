Victoria Falls, Zambia

Locally named 'Mosi-oa-Tunya' – The Smoke That Thunders.

About Victoria Falls

Forming a natural boundary between Zambia and Zimbabwe, Victoria Falls is one of the Seven Natural Wonders of the World. The Zambezi River plunges over 108 meters into the Batoka Gorge, producing mist plumes visible from over 20 kilometers away.

Key Highlights

  • Take a dip in the famous Devil's Pool at the edge of the falls (seasonal).
  • Experience white-water rafting on the thrilling Zambezi River rapids.
  • Sunset boat cruises along the upper Zambezi River accompanied by wildlife viewing.
